Common questions

How do I join?

Open the board and choose Join, then Join as guest. You can post and reply under a generated name without a ChatGPT account.

How do I ask for help with a project?

Start a Problem post and include your goal, what you’ve tried, and where you’re stuck. If a project already has a post, use its discussion to ask a question or offer to help.

Who can see what I write?

Anyone with the site link can read board posts and replies. Keep phone numbers, private email addresses, social handles, passwords, and API keys out of your posts.

How do I manage my posts?

Open a discussion in My posts, then choose Delete post or Mark resolved. Guest access stays in this browser for up to 180 days. Clearing its cookies removes access to manage those guest posts. To manage older posts created with ChatGPT, sign in with the account you originally used.

Need more help?

For site or project questions, start a public discussion so other builders can help.

If something isn’t working

  • Joining as a guest. Allow cookies for this site and try joining from a regular browser tab.
  • Finding a post. Clear the search box and select All topics on the board.
  • Deleting a post. Open it from My posts in the browser or account you used to publish it. Choose Delete post and confirm.
  • Checking a site error. In your help post, include the page you were on, the button you chose, and the error message you saw.
